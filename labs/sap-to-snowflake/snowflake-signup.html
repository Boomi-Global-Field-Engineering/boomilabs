Snowflake Free Trial: Step-by-Step Signup Guide

Create a free Snowflake trial account — the first step before the SAP to Snowflake lab. The trial is completely free, requires no credit card, and includes $400 in free credits valid for 30 days.

Back to Exercise 2
What you get with the free trial
  • $400 in free credits — equivalent to substantial compute and storage usage
  • 30-day access — full platform access from the signup date
  • Enterprise Edition features — multi-cluster warehouses, materialized views, Time Travel up to 90 days, and more
  • Pre-provisioned learning environment — a role, warehouse, and database ready to use immediately after login
  • Snowflake Marketplace access — 150+ public datasets at no extra cost

1Navigate to the Signup Page

Open a browser (Chrome is recommended) and go to signup.snowflake.com. You will land on the Snowflake free trial registration page.

2Enter Your Personal Details

Fill out the registration form with the following information:

  • First Name and Last Name
  • Work or personal email address — used to activate your account and log in
  • Company name — enter "Self" or any organization name for learning purposes
  • Country or region — may be auto-detected based on your location

No mobile number is required.

3Choose Your Cloud Platform

Select one of three cloud providers to host your Snowflake account: AWS, Microsoft Azure, or Google Cloud Platform.

Provider cannot be changed laterYour cloud provider cannot be changed after account creation. If you need a different provider, you will have to create a new trial account. For this lab, AWS is recommended.

4Select Your Region

After choosing a cloud provider, select the cloud region closest to your physical location — a nearby region gives the best query performance during the lab.

  • The platform may auto-select a region based on your IP address.
  • For most North America participants on AWS, US West (Oregon) or US East (N. Virginia) are common choices.
Region cannot be changed laterLike the cloud provider, the region cannot be changed after account creation.

5Select Your Snowflake Edition

Snowflake offers multiple editions. For hands-on labs, Enterprise is strongly recommended — it ensures all lab exercises work as expected, including advanced features like multi-cluster warehouses and extended Time Travel.

6Accept Terms and Submit

  • Check the box to accept Snowflake's Terms of Service.
  • Click Get Started (or the equivalent submit button).

You will see a confirmation screen indicating your signup is complete.

7Activate Your Account via Email

Within a few minutes, you will receive an activation email from Snowflake.

  • Check your inbox for an email from Snowflake.
  • If not found, check your spam or junk folder.
  • Click the Click to Activate button in the email.

If the email doesn't arrive after several minutes, verify the address you entered and request a new activation email. Corporate email filters may block Snowflake — ask IT to whitelist Snowflake email domains if needed.

8Create Your Username and Password

After clicking the activation link, you are taken to the account setup page:

  • Choose a username (this is your Snowflake login name — save it).
  • Set a strong password.
  • Click Get started or the equivalent confirmation button.

9Log In to Snowsight

After completing account setup, you are automatically logged into — or redirected to — Snowsight, Snowflake's web-based UI. You can also log in any time at app.snowflake.com.

Enter the account URL included in your activation email (bookmark it for easy access), along with your username and password.

10Explore Your Trial Environment

Once logged in, your Snowflake trial environment is ready. A pre-provisioned COMPUTE_WH virtual warehouse and default databases are available from the moment you log in — you're ready to begin Exercise 2.

Trial limitations & tips
  • Credits are consumed whenever a warehouse is running, even if no queries execute — warehouses auto-suspend after inactivity to save credits.
  • Don't set warehouses to 4X-Large or run them without auto-suspend during the lab — this burns through credits quickly.
  • Some features (external network access, hybrid tables) have limited daily credit usage until a payment method is added.
  • The trial is suspended after 30 days or when credits are exhausted — add a credit card under Admin > Billing to convert to a paid account and preserve your data.
  • You can create multiple trial accounts with different email addresses if you need a fresh environment.
