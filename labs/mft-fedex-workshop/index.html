FedEx Freight MFT Workshop

Three real-world use cases captured directly from a FedEx Freight discovery session. Maps their current FedEx Net workflow to Boomi MFT — covering banking file transfer, multi-destination HR files, and internal invoice image distribution.

~90 minutes 4 modules Advanced
Discovery context: This lab is based on a live discovery session with FedEx Freight's MFT team (Chastity & Nicole). Their current system is FedEx Net — a legacy homegrown MFT platform. The goal is to map each use case to Boomi MFT ahead of a Wednesday POC workshop.

Internal to External — Single Destination (Banking)

CDAS generates a weekly banking file that FedEx Net pushes to Scotia Bank via SFTP. Maps to Boomi MFT as a scheduled push with SSH key auth and full audit trail.

Internal to External — Multiple Destinations (HR & Workday)

Workday and Ab Initio servers push HR files into FedEx Net, which fans out to multiple banking SFTP servers hourly. Files range from 250KB to 7GB; all HR files are encrypted.

Fan-Out Pattern Workday Ab Initio Encrypted HR Files

Internal to Internal — Invoice Image & Mailbox Distribution

Invoice Image (bills of lading, up to 45GB) drops files into a FedEx Net mailbox. Hundreds of external and internal receivers pull their own files via SFTP GET using isolated user accounts.

POC Context & Blockers

  • VPN blocker: FedEx Freight's new VPN update is blocking access to the Boomi MFT cloud. IP whitelisting is in progress (expected same day or next morning).
  • Hard deadline: POC must close by end of week. Leadership will not extend. Plan for Boomi-hosted demo if VPN access isn't resolved.
  • No system access: Team is locked out of CDAS credentials. Demo will need to simulate file sources rather than connect live.
  • Workshop goal: Wednesday session should answer — "how many clicks does Boomi take away?" Show partner onboarding, destination setup, and error recovery vs. FedEx Net's 17-click manual process.
