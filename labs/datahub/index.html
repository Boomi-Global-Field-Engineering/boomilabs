Boomi Data Hub — Hands-on Lab

Build a complete Data Hub solution end-to-end — define a Model, configure match rules and data quality, attach Salesforce and Netsuite as sources, fix data quality errors, and synchronize consolidated golden records out to a marketing database.

~2 hours Intermediate 4 Exercises

Exercises

  • 1

    Setup & Foundation

    Log in to Data Hub and create the central data Model that will define the structure of records flowing into the hub.

    ~15 min 2 steps
  • 2

    Configure the Model

    Add governance to the Model — match rules to identify duplicates, data-quality steps to validate inbound records, and tags to track lifecycle. Then publish and deploy.

    ~25 min 4 steps
  • 3

    Connect Sources

    Attach source systems (Salesforce, Netsuite) to the Model and run upsert flows that bring records into the hub.

    ~50 min 3 steps
  • 4

    Operations

    Fix a data quality error and synchronize the consolidated golden record set out to a downstream marketing database.

    ~30 min 2 steps

Prerequisites

  • Access to Boomi AtomSphere (platform.boomi.com) — credentials provided by your trainer
  • Access to Data Hub from the AtomSphere services menu
  • Basic familiarity with the Boomi AtomSphere canvas (drag, drop, configure shapes)
  • Source-system credentials for Salesforce and Netsuite — provided by your trainer for Exercises 3 and 4
