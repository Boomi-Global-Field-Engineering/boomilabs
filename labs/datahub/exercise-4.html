Step 2 of 2

Synchronise to Marketing DB

Check that there are records to be synchronised to the Marketing DB.

Go to "Reporting" 🡪 "Outbound Activity":

Notice that there are a couple of entries:

We are going to configure a process which takes these entries and synchronises them to the Marketing DB. To save time copy the process "Sync to Marketing DB" in the "SHARED" folder:

Make sure "Copy Component Dependents" is ticked and choose your User folder:

Click "OK".

Navigate to your User folder and double click on the process:

Click on "Configure" on the start shape:

Click "+" to add a new operation:

Name the operation "Query New Prospect". Click "Import":

Choose the "Boomi MDM" connection under SHARED:

Enter "marketing_db_YOUR-NAME" in the source field. Click "Next":

Choose your customer object:

Click "Finish":

Click "Save and Close":

Click "OK":

Go back to the process and click on the Map shape:

Click on "Lock & Edit":

Click on "Choose" in the Map Source:

Select Profile Type "XML" then under your User folder select "Boomi Data Hub customer<yourname> QUERY Response". Click "OK":

Map from left to right as shown below (you may like to use Boomi Suggest):

Click "Save and Close".

On the process click "Save":

Click "Test":

Choose "workshop-atom" then click "Run Test":

Ensure that you have green highlights on the shapes:

Go back to the "Outbound Activity" page, click the refresh button. There should now be no entries. This means that the data has now sync’d with the Marketing DB:
